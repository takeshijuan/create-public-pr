# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive disagreement, protect personal and private information, and focus feedback on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private maintainer contact or security-advisory feature. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
